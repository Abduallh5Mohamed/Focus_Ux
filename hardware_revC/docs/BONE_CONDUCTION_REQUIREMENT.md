# Required output update - integrated non-implantable bone conduction

The human has clarified that the product must use integrated bone-conduction output. Previous RevC models omit this subsystem and are therefore incomplete even apart from the failed hinge-motion tests. Ear sleeves and nose-pad references in that CAD are NOT audio transducers. Do not relabel them as speakers.

## Intended architecture
Input capture on glasses -> phone gateway -> GPU service -> decoded/limited audio -> validated driver/amplifier -> bone-conduction contact transducer(s) on the frame. Two contact units are a design option, not automatically needed or fitted to every user's hearing.

A phone-side wired USB-audio DAC/amplifier path is the first bench option. A fully integrated wireless-return option requires real RX/decode/DAC/I2S amplifier design and a power/thermal budget. ESP32-S3 supports BLE, not Bluetooth Classic A2DP or LE Audio; do not assume a consumer headset profile is available merely because the module has Bluetooth. Returned PCM over WiFi is another candidate, but concurrent A/V uplink + audio downlink/resource usage/timestamps need measurement.

## Mechanical/electrical changes required before the next CAD freeze
- Select the exact transducer and manufacturer drawing; reserve actual volume, mass, cable routing and contacts. No generic small bead is proof of useful bone output.
- Engineer a compliant contact mount on an appropriate bony area near the ear. Position, force, contact material and adjustment range must be validated on the intended users, not inferred from one head width.
- Add a matched driver, power source, startup mute, gain/output limits and fault/disconnect behavior. Do not connect an8ohm transducer to GPIO. Nominal electrical power is not a clinically safe listening level.
- Isolate transducer vibration from the capture mic and test mechanical/acoustic feedback; the same frame otherwise couples playback into input.
- Re-run static/dynamic clearance, hinge/cable tests, mass balance, surface temperature and comfort. RevC already fails folding and needs that defect fixed as part of the same revision.
- Validate actual vibrational output/frequency response using appropriate measurement equipment and qualified hearing expertise before efficacy/safe-use claims. Airborne SPL marketing values do not establish bone-output performance.

## Candidate for bench exploration only
Adafruit1674 is a published8ohm1W transducer, footprint14x21.5mm, mass9.6g. Two units add19.2g before mounts/wiring/driver; this is a mass warning, not a selected final wearable part. Height and actual coupling surface need the drawing/sample. The manufacturer recommends an audio amplifier. Do not buy/freeze this as the final output solely because it is available.

## Health scope
Non-implantable prototype output is not equivalent to an implanted bone-anchored hearing device. Bone conduction still stimulates the cochlea; it is not a blanket solution for cochlear/nerve hearing loss. Clinical candidacy varies. Clinical professional guidance on implanted devices must not be presented as validation of this DIY prototype.
